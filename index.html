About

Trader first. Teacher by choice.

I'm Hans Felix, known to most of my students as SirHans. I trade the markets every week, and I've spent years teaching traders what took me the longest to learn: strategy is only part of it. My bootcamps usually don't touch entries until day two or three, because discipline, patience and risk come first.

Through Markets Apprentice Academy, my free Telegram channel and the Sunday webinar, I break down live charts the way I actually trade them, wins and losses included.

Structure

Read the 4-hour chart first. Know where demand and supply really sit before you look for an entry.

Sessions

Use Asia, London and New York to time entries and place stops where price has already been tested.

Mindset

Make a decision, manage it, and live with it. Protect profit before you chase the last few points.
